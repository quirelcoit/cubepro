SPEEDCUBING CHEAT SHEET

What do EO, CO, CP, and EP mean?

Solving the Last Layer (LL) is divided into Orientation (making yellow face UP) and Permutation (moving pieces to matching sides):

EO • Edge Orientation

Turns yellow edges so yellow faces UP. Forms the Yellow Cross (Dot → L-Shape → Bar → Cross).

CO • Corner Orientation

Twists yellow corners so yellow faces UP. Turns the cross into a solid yellow face (Sune, Anti-Sune, etc.).

EO + CO = OLL
CP • Corner Permutation

Moves the 4 corners to their matching side colors. Look for matching Headlights (A-Perm, T-Perm).

EP • Edge Permutation

Cycles the final 3-4 edges into place to complete the entire cube (Ua, Ub, H, Z Permutations).

CP + EP = PLL

3 Levels of White Cross Solutions

The White Cross sets up your entire solve. Master your current level and level up when ready:

LEVEL 1 • ABSOLUTE BEGINNER

The "Daisy" Method (Petals to Top)

  • Idea: Don't worry about centers yet! Bring all 4 white edge stickers around the Yellow center to form a daisy flower.
  • Step A: Edge in middle layer → F or R to lift to top.
  • Step B: Edge on bottom with white down → F2 straight to top.
  • Step C: Once all 4 petals are on yellow, match side color to center and turn 180° (F2, R2, B2, L2) to deposit onto white bottom.
⏱️ 10–15s • Cube flipping • Zero stress, 100% reliable for day 1.
LEVEL 2 • INTERMEDIATE

Direct Bottom Cross (Skip the Daisy)

  • Idea: Keep White on BOTTOM from second zero! Never flip the cube upside down.
  • Rule 1: Middle layer edges insert directly onto bottom in 1 turn (F, R', L, B).
  • Rule 2: Flipped top edge inserts directly in 3 moves with F' U L'.
  • Rule 3: Use Keyhole (D turntable) to protect already-placed cross edges without breaking them.
  • Rule 4: Swap adjacent edges with R2 D' R2 D R2 or opposite with R2 L2 D2 R2 L2.
⚡ 4–6s • Solved directly on bottom • Saves 8 seconds per solve.
LEVEL 3 • PRO & ADVANCED

Sub-20 CFOP • 15s Inspection & BOOG

  • The B-O-O-G Rule: Clockwise around white is Blue → Orange → Green → Red. Place edges in relative order, then ONE D aligns all 4 centers simultaneously!

HACK 2 • PRO EDGE CONTROL

Sledgehammer Insert

Instead of doing standard U (R U' R') into the front-right slot, do the Sledgehammer:

(R' F R F')
🔥 Why it's OP: It inserts the pair AND flips edges yellow! Guarantees an easy yellow cross (skips the slow Dot OLL).
HACK 3 • ZERO ROTATIONS

Back-Slot Preference

Always solve the back two slots first when pairs appear:

Back-Right: R' U' R • Back-Left: L U L'
💡 Keeps the front slots empty and visible. Eliminates 90% of cube rotations (y / y')!
HACK 4 • PRO CHEAT CODE

The Keyhole Method

Leave 1 corner unsolved on purpose! Move the D layer so that empty slot is directly underneath where you insert middle edges:

R U R' or F' U' F
✨ Solves middle edges in 3 simple moves without disturbing anything else.

FINGERTRICK MASTERY

Ergonomics & Flow: Efficiency Over Raw Spam

Beginners often compensate for hesitant lookahead with frantic, messy turning. Speed champions win with effortless ergonomics and low move counts:

1. The Right Index Flick (U)

Never rotate your right hand. Rest your right index finger at the back-right corner and pull forward like a trigger.

2. The Double Flick (U2)

Right index flick followed immediately by right middle finger flick in one smooth wave. Cut your U2 time by 70%!

3. The Sexy Move Rhythm (R U R' U')

Right wrist up (R), index flick (U), right wrist down (R'), left index push (U'). Drill this until it takes under 0.7 seconds.

4. Sledgehammer (R' F R F')

Right wrist down, thumb pushes F up, right wrist up, index pushes F' down. Used for orienting edges and F2L inserts without rotations.

BEGINNER ZERO-JARGON GUIDE

What do R, U, F, and (') Prime mean?

Each letter represents a face: R=Right, L=Left, U=Upper (Top), D=Down (Bottom), F=Front, B=Back.
• Letter alone (e.g. R): Turn that face CLOCKWISE (90°).
• Apostrophe ' (Prime, e.g. R'): Turn it COUNTER-CLOCKWISE (the other way).
• Number 2 (e.g. U2): Turn it TWICE (180°).
• Lowercase f: Push FRONT TWO LAYERS together.

R
Right UP ⬆️

Push the right side upwards away from you with your right hand.

R'
Right DOWN ⬇️

Pull the right side downwards towards you.

U
Top LEFT ⬅️

Flick the top face to the left using your right index finger.

U'
Top RIGHT ➡️

Flick the top face to the right using your left index finger.

F
Front Clockwise ↻

Turn the front face like clock hands turning forward.

F'
Front Counter-Clockwise ↺

Turn the front face backwards counter-clockwise.

f
Front TWO Layers ↻

Push both the front face and middle slice together clockwise.

r
Right TWO Layers ⬆️

Push both the right face and middle slice upwards together.

L / L'
Left Side (L=Down, L'=Up)

Clockwise for left face pushes DOWN towards you; counter-clockwise pushes UP.

D / D'
Bottom Face (Underneath)

Turn the bottom layer with your ring finger from underneath.

M / M'
Middle Slice

The slice between Left and Right. M pushes down, M' pushes up.

U2 / R2
Double Turn (180°)

Turn the face twice. Double-flick with index then middle finger.

(R' F R F') • The Sledgehammer ⚡
