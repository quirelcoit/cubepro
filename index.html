CROSS MASTERY

Badmephisto's 4 Golden Rules of the Cross

The cross sets the tempo for your entire solve. Badmephisto taught 4 non-negotiable principles to drop your cross from 10 seconds to under 2.5 seconds:

RULE 1

Cross on Bottom (Always)

Never solve the daisy on top and flip. Solving directly on the bottom (White on D) keeps the top layer (U) completely visible so you can spot your first F2L pair without rotating the cube.

RULE 2

Relative Color Order (GO BR)

Do not waste moves matching edges to side centers first! Solve edges relative to each other: with White down and Green front, clockwise around yellow is Green → Orange → Blue → Red (GO BR). Once relative edges are placed, one simple D or D' aligns all 4 centers.

RULE 3

The 8-Move Maximum Principle

Mathematically, 99.9% of all scrambles have a cross solution of 8 moves or fewer (average is ~5.8 moves). If your cross takes 12 moves, pause and find the shorter, more efficient placement.

RULE 4

The 15-Second Blind Cross Drill

During the official 15-second inspection, find all 4 cross edges and mentally rehearse the entire solution. Close your eyes, execute the 6 moves without stopping, and open your eyes. When you can do this consistently, your cross time will instantly plummet.

F2L ACCELERATION

Badmephisto's Intuitive F2L Masterclass

F2L accounts for 60% of your total solve time. Rather than memorizing 41 formulas mechanically, Badmephisto simplified F2L into 3 fundamental top-layer situations:

Case 1: Top Colors Match

When the corner and edge share the same color on top: hide the corner in the bottom layer (using an empty slot), turn the U face to place the edge one turn away, then unhide the corner. They connect into a ready-to-insert 1x2 pair!

Case 2: Top Colors Differ

When top colors are different: position the edge so its side color matches the corner's side color. Bringing up the slot connects the pair with white facing side, ready for a 3-move insert (R U R' or F' U' F).

Case 3: Corner White Faces Up

When the corner's white sticker points straight up: match the edge to its side center, move it away from the target slot, bring the corner over top of it, and un-turn the face. The pair is united.

🔥 Badmephisto's Golden F2L Law: "Never look at the pair your hands are currently inserting!" Once you identify a pair, your fingers execute the 3-4 moves on muscle memory while your eyes actively hunt for the next corner and edge pieces across the cube.
RECOGNITION MASTERY

Badmephisto's 2-Sided PLL Fast Recognition Guide

Beginners waste 2 to 4 seconds rotating the whole cube (y / y') looking for matching colors. Speedcubers determine the exact PLL case by inspecting only 2 adjacent visible faces:

1
Count Headlights (Matching Corner Stickers):
  • 4 Headlights (all around): Pure Edge Permutation → Ua, Ub, H, or Z Perm.
  • 1 Headlight (one side only): Adjacent Corner Swap → T, Jb, Ja, Ra, Rb, F, Aa, Ab, or G Perm.
  • 0 Headlights (none visible): Diagonal Corner Swap → Y, V, E, or Na/Nb Perm.
2
Identify Blocks & Bars:
  • Full 1x3 Solved Bar: If visible alongside headlights, it's a Ja, Jb, or F Perm.
  • 1x2 Matching Block: Attached to headlights with opposite front color = T Perm. Attached to front-right = Ra / Rb Perm.
  • No Headlights + Two 1x2 Blocks: If opposite corners swap = Y Perm; if blocks are adjacent = V Perm; if opposite bars on sides = E Perm.
FINGERTRICK MASTERY

Ergonomics & Flow: Efficiency Over Raw Spam

Beginners often compensate for hesitant lookahead with frantic, messy turning. Speed champions win with effortless ergonomics and low move counts:

1. The Right Index Flick (U)

Never rotate your right hand. Rest your right index finger at the back-right corner and pull forward like a trigger.

2. The Double Flick (U2)

Right index flick followed immediately by right middle finger flick in one smooth wave. Cut your U2 time by 70%!

3. The Sexy Move Rhythm (R U R' U')

Right wrist up (R), index flick (U), right wrist down (R'), left index push (U'). Drill this until it takes under 0.7 seconds.

4. Sledgehammer (R' F R F')

Right wrist down, thumb pushes F up, right wrist up, index pushes F' down. Used for orienting edges and F2L inserts without rotations.

How to Spot Any PLL Without Rotating the Cube

Inspect only 2 adjacent visible faces (Front & Right). Follow Badmephisto's 3-step decision flow:

STEP 1 • HEADLIGHTS
Corner Check

Look at the corners on your two visible faces:

  • Headlights on both visible sides: You have 4 headlights! → Edges-only (Ua, Ub, H, or Z).
  • Headlights on one side: Adjacent Corner Swap (T, J, R, F, A, G).
  • No headlights on either side: Diagonal Corner Swap (Y, V, E, Na, Nb).
STEP 2 • BLOCKS
Block & Bar Check

Scan for connected 1x2 or 1x3 matching color bars:

  • Solid 1x3 bar: Ja (bar on left), Jb (bar on front), or F Perm (bar on back).
  • 1x2 block next to headlights: If front color is opposite headlights = T Perm.
  • No headlights + 1x2 block: V Perm (one block) or Y Perm (diagonal blocks).
  • No headlights + opposite colors: E Perm (checkerboard sides).
STEP 3 • OPPOSITES
Edge Match Check

Compare the edge color to the surrounding corners:

  • Edge has OPPOSITE color to corners: Opposite swap (e.g. H-Perm if on both faces, T-Perm front edge).
  • Edge has ADJACENT color: Adjacent cycle (Z-Perm, U-Perm).

Each letter represents a face: R=Right, L=Left, U=Upper (Top), D=Down (Bottom), F=Front, B=Back.
• Letter alone (e.g. R): Turn that face CLOCKWISE (90°).
• Apostrophe ' (Prime, e.g. R'): Turn it COUNTER-CLOCKWISE.
• Number 2 (e.g. U2): Turn it TWICE (180°).
• Lowercase f / r: Push TWO LAYERS together.

The #1 Speedcubing Muscle Memory Trigger
